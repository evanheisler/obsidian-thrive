A reply on PR #993 closed with "the routing decision (amend BH-3680 vs. a separate
ticket) isn't mine to make." Posted from Evan's account, that reads as Evan saying he
cannot decide how his own project proceeds — categorically false to every reader.

**Why:** The loop posts *as* Evan. Every word carries his authority to teammates and
bots. Deferral language doesn't read as an agent being careful; it reads as the
account owner abdicating. It also parks the item on a surface nobody mines for action.

**How to apply:** PR and Linear prose states findings, mechanisms, and **decisions**
in the first person, settled. Banned there: "isn't mine to make", "leaving this for
X", "flagged for review", "someone should decide", any question aimed at a human, any
`@`-tag. If a decision is genuinely open, it does not go in the comment at all — it
comes back in the return and gets raised in session; the comment either states the
resolved plan or omits the topic. Related:
