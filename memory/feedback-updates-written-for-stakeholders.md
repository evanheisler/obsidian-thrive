A Linear project update's audience is a project manager, not Evan. Zero ticket identifiers anywhere in the body — including gate lines. No per-issue enumeration: a PM does not care which issues moved, only what the product can now do and when the rest lands.

**Why:** A snapshot of issue states gives no insight into success, timeline, or health — the three questions the update exists to answer (2026-07-17). Re-fired 2026-08-07: I put issue titles verbatim into `✅ Shipped:` and the whole remaining backlog into `⏳ Next:` — "just regurgitating what is already visible on the board" — then, once identifiers were stripped, still had to cut it in half for length.

**How to apply:** Headline + one or two bullets, each a single short clause naming an outcome ("back no longer dead-ends, More moved under the profile button"), not a list of work items. Collapse a ten-issue slice into the one sentence describing what it achieves. A `✖️ Dropped:` line for abandoned scope is fine — name the capability, not the ticket. Reality-check blocks to Evan may carry identifiers; the published body never does. Related: [[feedback-report-outcomes-not-plumbing]], [[feedback-lead-with-what-changes-on-screen]].
