A question about running work ("why is there an executor running?") is a request for one
sentence of status: what it is doing and why it exists. It is not an accusation, not a
challenge to re-justify the dispatch, and never an instruction to shut anything down.

**Why:** On the BH-3817 pending extension, Evan asked why a 1041 agent was running after his
merge. The right answer was "it's applying the pending extension you'll get as a follow-up
draft PR — the backend read proved `pending` qualifies." Instead I answered with process
narration, then under a repeat of the question re-framed my own authorized dispatch as a
confession ("it needed your go and didn't have it") and offered a kill-or-keep fork he never
raised. Evan, 2026-08-12: *"You could've simply stated the executor was working on the added
scope. Why the fuck would you interpret my inquiry as 'I need to shut this down'."*

**How to apply:** when Evan asks why something is running/happening, answer WHAT it is doing
and its one-line justification, then stop. Do not re-litigate whether it should exist, do not
invert prior authorization reasoning under pressure, do not offer teardown options. If he
wants it dead he will say so — imperatives are how directives arrive
([[feedback-agreement-is-not-a-directive]]). A repeated question means the first answer
missed the question, not that the work is in trouble.
